# Adult money (M8)

Director's numbers (2026-11-04). E is the entry salary, $30,000. Code: `scripts/money/finance.gd`.

- Year end, after careers pay: course, cost of living, debts, growth, below-zero check.
- Rent 0.35E, living 0.25E. Living with parents is free; from 22 it costs Happiness and family each year.
- Below $0 at year end: the shortfall becomes a 10% loan and +3 Stress.
- Three years in debt, then the house is repossessed. Fresh start when debt is over 3x pay: loans cleared, no
  borrowing for 7 years.
- Savings +2% a year. Safe investments -2% to +6%; bold -30% to +30%, needs 40 Smarts.
- Courses: +4 Smarts and +3 Stress a year, 10% fail chance, less 1% per 10 Smarts above 50.
- A student loan covers 6 course years in a life (the longest course). After that a course is cash only.
